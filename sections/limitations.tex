\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Calibration and labels.} The controller needs an operator label and a per-domain calibration of the frontier and the learning curve, and it does not train without them. \Cref{sec:robust} bounds the cost of a stale or missing label on fixed mixtures; a mixture whose proportion drifts within a regime is not covered.

\paragraph{Prediction error and engine overhead.} The controller acts on predicted throughput and TPOT, not on measured latency (\cref{app:controller}); where the drafter's ceiling sits at break-even, as on the code--chat mixture, a frontier error of a few percent decides whether training pays. The switch can gain at most what the engine's speculation-off state costs: \nv{zh.switch_vs_plain.abs}--\nv{code.switch_vs_plain.abs}\% below plain decoding in vLLM, but \nv{sgl.off_vs_plain.c256}\% in SGLang, where plain decoding therefore stays ahead on Chinese (\cref{sec:sglang}).

\paragraph{Scope.} We evaluate two engines (vLLM 0.27.1 and, with five policies, SGLang 0.5.10), one GPU type (H100), two targets of one model family, EAGLE-3 drafters with $k{=}3$, greedy decoding with natural stopping at up to 512 tokens (\cref{tab:protocol}), single-GPU serving without prefix caching, and no tensor-parallel evaluation of the controller; \pol{TIDE} reimplements only TIDE's training triggers on a shared GPU, whereas TIDE trains on separate GPUs; the live workload is a synthetic chat--new-domain--chat sequence, and other traffic shapes are covered only by two fixed mixtures and in simulation.

\paragraph{Statistics and development.} Each cell has three paired repetitions on \nv{hosts} nodes of the same type; node interference slowed \nv{scan.flagged.word} baseline runs, replaced by reruns (\cref{app:rerun}), smaller slowdowns remain, and paired differences of a few percent between adaptive policies are within the run-to-run spread. The rules and constants were developed on runs from the same prompt pools in different orders; the frozen evaluation therefore does not test them on unseen prompts.
